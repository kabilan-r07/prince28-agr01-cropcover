# CropCover – Future Test Questions

This document contains 10 test questions designed to evaluate the accuracy and usefulness of the CropCover application.

## 1. Crop Insurance Eligibility

**Question:** Is paddy cultivation covered under PMFBY in Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu?

**Expected Result:** The application should check the applicable season notification and explain whether paddy is eligible for insurance.

## 2. Crop Damage Due to Heavy Rain

**Question:** My samba paddy crop was damaged due to heavy rainfall. Can I claim crop insurance?

**Expected Result:** The application should explain the potentially applicable claim category and eligibility conditions.

## 3. Localised Calamity

**Question:** My agricultural field was flooded, but nearby fields were not affected. Which type of insurance claim should I apply for?

**Expected Result:** The application should explain localised calamity coverage and its applicable conditions.

## 4. Claim Reporting Deadline

**Question:** How many hours do I have to report crop damage under the applicable PMFBY claim rules?

**Expected Result:** The application should explain the applicable 72-hour intimation requirement and its conditions, with an official source.

## 5. Premium Calculation

**Question:** What is the farmer's premium for insuring one hectare of paddy?

**Expected Result:** The application should retrieve the applicable premium rate and calculate the amount using the correct notified sum insured.

## 6. Sum Insured

**Question:** What is the sum insured for paddy cultivation in Nagapattinam district?

**Expected Result:** The application should retrieve the correct value from the relevant Tamil Nadu crop notification.

## 7. Drought Damage

**Question:** My paddy crop was damaged because of drought. Is it covered under PMFBY?

**Expected Result:** The application should explain applicable drought-related coverage and conditions.

## 8. Claim Application Process

**Question:** What documents and details are required to report crop damage and submit an insurance claim?

**Expected Result:** The application should provide the applicable reporting procedure, required information, and official guidance.

## 9. Weather-Based Insurance

**Question:** What is the difference between PMFBY and the Weather-Based Crop Insurance Scheme (RWBCIS)?

**Expected Result:** The application should explain the differences in coverage and claim assessment methods.

## 10. Claim Status and Rejection

**Question:** What should I do if my crop insurance claim is rejected?

**Expected Result:** The application should explain the applicable grievance and complaint procedures with official references.

---

## Testing Objectives

- Verify the correctness of crop insurance information.
- Check claim classification accuracy.
- Verify premium and sum insured calculations.
- Test claim deadline information.
- Ensure answers contain relevant official government references.

**Target:** Test all 10 questions and expand the test dataset to at least 30 crop-district scenarios.
